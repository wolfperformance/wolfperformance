FAQ — ECU remap Etobicoke

Do you come to Etobicoke?

Yes. Wolf Performance is a mobile service, so we come to you in Etobicoke: at home, at work or wherever the car is parked safely. Book online or call (416) 839-2266 with your car and your area.

How is an ECU remap different from a plug-in tuner box?

A remap changes the calibration inside the ECU itself, built around your car and goals, rather than altering sensor signals from outside. The work is done with professional-grade tuning equipment.

Can you tune a car with aftermarket parts?

Usually, yes. Tell us what is installed when you book. We confirm the parts and the car's health with diagnostics before calibrating.
