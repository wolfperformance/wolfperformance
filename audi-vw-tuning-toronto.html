Audi & VW platforms

Audi & VW engines and gearboxes we tune

Most Audi and VW performance cars share engines and gearboxes, which makes their behaviour well understood. The platforms we see most:

  • EA888 2.0 TSI / TFSI: Golf GTI, Golf R, Jetta GLI, Audi A3, S3, A4, A5, Q5, and VW Tiguan and Atlas 2.0T.
  • EA839 2.9 and 3.0 TFSI V6: Audi S4, S5, SQ5, S6, S7 and RS5.
  • EA855 2.5 TFSI five-cylinder: Audi RS3 and TT RS, a favourite for its sound and its appetite for good calibration.
  • 4.0 TFSI V8: RS6, RS7, RS Q8 and S8, the performance-tier V8s.
  • DSG and S tronic gearboxes: DQ250, DQ381 and DQ500 wet-clutch units, the DL501 and DL382, plus the ZF 8HP in S4, S5 and SQ5.

Not on the list? Send us the year, model and engine. If the software can be calibrated safely, we will tell you what is possible and what it costs before anything is booked.

What Stage 1, Stage 2 and TCU mean on a Audi & VW

Stage 1: software only

Software only, for the car as it sits. On EA888 cars that means a stronger, smoother mid-range, a cleaner throttle in every drive mode and torque limits that make sense for the clutch. On V6, five-cylinder and V8 cars it brings sharper response without changing how civil the car is at low speed.

Stage 2: built around your parts

Built around supporting hardware, typically an upgraded intake, intercooler, turbo inlet or downpipe. The calibration is matched to the parts and logged for fuelling and intake temperatures. Installs are by referral for now; we calibrate around them.

TCU: the gearbox to match

DSG and S tronic gearboxes have clamping and torque limits that need to match the engine. A TCU calibration raises them, shortens shift times, improves launch behaviour where supported, and can make the gearbox hold gears properly in manual mode.

FAQ: Audi & VW tuning

Do you tune GTIs and Golf Rs at home?

Yes. We come to your home, work or driveway with professional-grade equipment, and there is no travel fee across Toronto, the GTA and Barrie.

Is the original software kept?

Yes. The factory map is saved before anything is written, so the car can go back to stock at any time.

Do I need a DSG tune too?

Not always, but on DSG cars it is recommended with an engine tune. TCU calibration is from $999, or from $1,799 bundled with Stage 1.

Are S4 and S5 priced as performance cars?

We quote V6 S models for your exact car and software. Audi RS models and V8s are in the performance tier, from $1,499 for Stage 1; EA888 cars such as the GTI, Golf R and S3 start from $999.

What does the health scan check?

Every module, live engine data and fault history. On direct-injection Audi and VW engines we also look for signs of misfires and boost leaks before tuning.
