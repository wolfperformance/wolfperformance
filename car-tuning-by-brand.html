FAQ

Which makes do you tune?

Most modern turbo and V8 cars and trucks, including Mercedes-AMG, BMW, Audi and Volkswagen, Ford, Chevrolet and Dodge/RAM, plus Porsche, Subaru, Toyota, Honda, Hyundai and Kia, Nissan and Infiniti, Jeep, Lexus and more. Send your year, model and engine and we will confirm support.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Wolf Performance is a mobile service: we come to your home, work or driveway across Toronto, the GTA and Barrie.

What does tuning cost?

Stage 1 is from $999, or from $1,499 for performance and V8 cars. Stage 2 is from $1,299, TCU calibration from $999 and the Stage 1 + TCU bundle from $1,799. Diagnostics and a health scan are from $99, credited toward any tune booked within 30 days.

Can my car go back to stock?

Yes. The original factory map is saved before anything is written.
