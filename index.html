What changes after a tune

The car you thought you bought.

A well-made calibration is felt every time you press the pedal, not just at full throttle. This is what a good tune is designed to change.

01

Sharper throttle response

Less lag between your foot and the car. Throttle mapping is calibrated so response feels immediate and predictable.

02

Stronger mid-range pull

The torque you use every day, merging onto the 401 or rolling on in third, is where a good calibration is felt most.

03

Smoother power delivery

Power builds cleanly instead of arriving in a lump. OEM+ smooth at part throttle, not just strong at full boost.

04

More confident overtakes

Passing on a two-lane road or merging into fast traffic feels calmer when the car pulls cleanly through the gears.

How much each one changes depends on your car, fuel and supporting mods. We tell you what is realistic for your platform before any work.

FAQ

Before you book.

Straight answers on the visit, safety, power and price.

Do you really come to me?

Yes. Wolf Performance is a mobile service. We come to your home, your workplace or wherever the car is parked safely across Toronto, the GTA and Barrie, so there is no drop-off and no waiting room.

Is ECU tuning safe for daily driving?

Done properly, yes. We prioritize reliability and thermal management, confirm the car is healthy first, and validate the result with logs.

Can I go back to stock?

Yes. Your original factory map is saved before we write anything, so the car can be returned to stock if you ever need to.

How much power will I gain?

It depends on the car, the engine, the fuel you run and its condition, so we don't quote a generic number. We review your car first and tell you honestly what is realistic before any work is done. Your original factory map is saved, so you can always go back to stock.

How much does it cost?

Starting prices (CAD): Stage 1 from $999; Stage 1 for performance and V8 cars from $1,499; Stage 2 from $1,299; TCU / gearbox tuning from $999; the Stage 1 + TCU bundle from $1,799; diagnostics and health scan from $99. The $99 health scan is credited toward any tune booked within 30 days. No travel fee across Toronto, the GTA and Barrie. Your exact price depends on the platform and what you want, so send your car through the quick quote form or call (416) 839-2266 and we quote for your car before anything is booked in.
