FAQ — performance tuning Cambridge

Do you come to Cambridge?

Yes. Wolf Performance is a mobile service, so we come to you in Cambridge: at home, at work or wherever the car is parked safely. Book online or call (416) 839-2266 with your car and your area. As Cambridge is further out, we confirm a visit date when you book.

What information should I send?

Year, make, model, engine, modifications, fuel, any fault codes and service history if you have it, plus your goal.

How much power can I expect?

It depends on the car, the engine, the fuel you run and its condition, so we do not quote a generic number. We review your car first and tell you honestly what is realistic before any work is done. Your original factory map is saved, so you can always go back to stock.

Can I book diagnostics on their own?

Yes. Full-system scans, live data and boost, smoke and electrical checks are available without a tune.
