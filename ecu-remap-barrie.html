Every ECU remap starts with diagnostics to confirm the car is mechanically sound. We then calibrate air-fuel, boost, ignition timing and related maps for your goals, whether that is OEM+ street manners or a stage progression with supporting mods.

TCU tuning that matches the power

Transmission control unit (TCU) optimization improves shift logic, clutch pressure management and responsiveness, so the driveline keeps up with the engine calibration.

  • Faster, cleaner shift behaviour
  • Smoother torque transfer
  • Improved everyday drivability

FAQ

Do you come to me in Barrie?

Yes. Wolf Performance is a mobile service, so we come to your home, work or driveway anywhere in Barrie and nearby. Book online or call (416) 839-2266 with your car and your area.

Is ECU tuning safe for daily driving?

Yes. Professional calibration prioritizes reliability and thermal management while improving performance, and we confirm the car is healthy before writing anything.

How much horsepower can I gain?

It depends on the car, the engine, the fuel you run and its condition, so we do not quote a generic number. We review your car first and tell you honestly what is realistic before any work is done. Your original factory map is saved, so you can always go back to stock.

Will tuning void my warranty?

Manufacturer policies vary. Always check with your dealership regarding warranty terms before modifying software.
