Calibrated for how Toronto cars are actually driven

Toronto driving is a mix of stop-and-go on the Gardiner, the DVP and the 401, short city hops that never fully warm an engine, and a winter of salt and cold starts. A good calibration has to stay smooth at low speed and part throttle, not just at full boost. That is why we log the car before and after, and why OEM+ drivability is the default goal rather than an afterthought.
