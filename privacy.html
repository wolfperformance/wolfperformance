Privacy policy

Plain-language summary of how Wolf Performance handles your personal information. Last updated October 10, 2026.

Who we are

Wolf Performance is a mobile ECU tuning and diagnostics service in Ontario, Canada. We are responsible for the personal information we collect. Questions about this policy go to info@wolfperformance.ca or (416) 839-2266.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through our forms, by email or by phone:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your vehicle (year, make, model) and the service you want
  • Your address or area, so we can come to you
  • Anything you write in the notes

Like most websites, our hosting provider may also log basic technical data, such as IP address and browser type, for security and reliability.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your request, give you a quote, arrange and carry out your visit, and keep a record of the work done on your vehicle. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for anything unrelated to your request.

Consent

By sending a form, email or message you consent to us using your information for the purposes above. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us; we will then stop using it, except where we must keep records by law.

Who else sees it

Our website forms are delivered by Formsubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service that forwards each submission to our email inbox. Our email and hosting providers store data on our behalf and may process it outside Canada, where it can be subject to local laws. We share information with anyone else only if the law requires it.

Marketing messages

We do not send promotional emails or texts unless you have clearly agreed to receive them, and every message would include a way to unsubscribe. Replying to your own request is not marketing.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, we will update this page first.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires for business records, and then delete it securely.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@wolfperformance.ca. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send payment card details through our forms.
