Modern cars are defined by their software. Getting more from them, or finding out what is wrong, takes digital precision, controlled calibration and data to back it up. That work does not need a shop: it needs the right tools, and we bring them to you.

FAQ

Can you diagnose my car at home?

Yes. Diagnostics are done on site with pro-grade scan tools: full-system scans, live data and fault-code checks, with or without a tune.

What can you do on site?

ECU remaps, TCU calibration where supported, diagnostics, scan and coding, pre-tune health checks, and data logging and refinement.

Do you still do exhaust work and engine builds?

Not on site. Exhaust fabrication, hardware installs, engine rebuilds and other heavier mechanical work need a lift, so for now those are by referral. We will tell you straight if your plan needs one.

Is ECU tuning safe for daily driving?

Yes. Professional calibration maintains reliability while improving performance, and every tune starts with a health check.
